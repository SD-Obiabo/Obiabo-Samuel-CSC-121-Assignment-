Frequently Asked Questions

How can I borrow a book?

Present your valid university identification card at the circulation desk and follow the borrowing procedure.

Can I access digital resources from home?

Where remote access is provided, students and staff can access approved digital resources using their university credentials.

How can I contact a librarian?

You can visit the library, call the library office, or use the contact form on this page.
