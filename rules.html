Library Rules & Regulations

Guidelines for a safe, quiet, and productive learning environment.

General Library Rules

01

Maintain Silence

Users must maintain silence in designated reading and study areas.

02

Library Identification

Students should carry their valid university identification cards when using the library.

03

Food and Drinks

Food and drinks are not permitted in the library except in designated areas.

04

Care of Library Materials

Books and other library materials must be handled carefully. Users must not damage, tear, or write in library materials.

05

Borrowed Materials

All borrowed materials must be returned on or before the stated due date.

06

Mobile Phones

Mobile phones should be placed on silent mode inside the library.

07

Computers and Internet

Library computers and internet facilities should be used primarily for academic and research purposes.

08

Personal Belongings

Users are responsible for their personal belongings. The library is not responsible for lost items.

09

Respect Library Staff

Users must treat library staff and other library users with respect and courtesy.

10

Follow Staff Instructions

Library users are expected to comply with reasonable instructions given by authorized library staff.

Borrowing Regulations

  • Only registered library users may borrow materials.
  • Borrowed materials must be returned by the due date.
  • Users are responsible for materials borrowed using their account.
  • Lost or damaged materials may be subject to replacement charges.
  • Reference materials may be restricted to library use only.
